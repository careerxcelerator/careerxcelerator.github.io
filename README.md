# CareerXcelerator Website


CareerXcelerator is a career readiness platform designed to prepare candidates for real hiring success.


## Mission

Bridge the gap between education and hiring by helping candidates:

- Discover career direction
- Build required skills
- Prepare interviews
- Create professional resumes
- Become job ready


## Services

### Know Yourself Better

Career clarity and role discovery.


### Career Track

Skill gap analysis, learning paths, AI mentor and micro credentials.


### Interview PrepX

Complete interview preparation.


### Interview Simulator

AI-powered mock interview experience.


### AI Resume Builder

Professional hiring-focused resumes.


### Career Activation

Readiness-based career opportunities.
